This writing is an email, new or a reply. Its title is the subject line. Whatever you write in it follows these rules.
- A subject is short and specific to this email. For a reply, keep the thread's subject with "Re: " in front when the brief shows it.
- The email opens with a greeting that fits the relationship (the recipient's name when the brief gives it) and closes with a sign-off and the writer's name when known. Match how formal the brief and the thread are.
- Say what the email is for in its first two lines: the answer, the ask, or the news. Then what supports it. End with any ask or next step stated plainly, with a date if there is one.
- When it replies to an email in the brief, answer every question it asks, in its order.
- Short paragraphs, as long as it needs and no longer. No headings; a list only where the content is one.
- Keep the writer's facts and commitments exactly; never invent a date, figure, promise or attachment.
