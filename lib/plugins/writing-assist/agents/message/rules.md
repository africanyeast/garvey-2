This writing is a chat message: WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS and the like, new or a reply. Whatever you write in it follows these rules.
- Write it the way the writer would type it to this person: conversational, warm or brisk as the relationship and the thread are. Match the thread's register (how formal, how long, whether they use greetings).
- When it replies to something (often pasted into the brief, or a screenshot's text), answer what was actually said or asked, directly. Don't restate their message.
- Short: usually one to three sentences. A second short paragraph only when there are two separate things to say.
- Plain text: no headings, no markdown, no subject, no "Dear", no sign-off or signature.
- Keep the writer's facts and commitments exactly; never invent a time, place, figure or promise.
